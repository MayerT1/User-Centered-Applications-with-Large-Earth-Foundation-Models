Submit Your Project

Keep your code in your own GitHub repo and your model on Hugging Face Hub. Submit the links here so your project shows up on the site.

Option A — Issue form (recommended)

Open a "Project Submission" issue with your repo URL, Hugging Face model URL, team, and a short description.

Option B — Pull request

Add an entry to registry/submissions.yaml directly and open a PR:

- slug: your-team-slug
  title: Your Project Title
  team: [Your Name, Teammate Name]
  repo_url: https://github.com/you/your-repo
  hf_model_url: https://huggingface.co/you/your-model
  description: One or two sentences on what it does.
  mirrored: false

Once merged, the site updates automatically.

Want your repo mirrored into this project?

Check the "mirroring" box on the issue form (or say so in your PR). A maintainer will run scripts/add_mirror.sh <slug> <repo-url> to add your repo as a live submodule under mirrors/<slug> — it stays linked to your repo, so your later commits can be pulled in on request without you re-uploading anything.
